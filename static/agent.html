    Before you upload your documents

      Your privacy

      This agent runs on this computer; the photos are not sent anywhere. It reads and checks documents — it does not match faces, check liveness or detect forgery.

      Hackathon build

      Use fictional sample documents only. Never upload a real identity document — yours or anyone else's.
